# Home — rysych.com

## Scope and mode
Homepage (`index.html`, `home.css`, `home.js`) and, in the same world, the five case pages (`case.css`, `assets/fundraise-case.css`) and `404.html`. Mode: Experience (portfolio). This is a redesign: the Glass Departures Board world is retired.

## Audience, job, proof
Hiring managers for Head of Design / Director of Product Design, often at California-style product companies. They must grasp role, promise and proof in one viewport, then open one or two cases. Proof: Admirals (CAC −67%, CTR ×3), Subskim (solo-built with AI, live), Mechanism (+28% onboarding; $300M+ portfolio for context), MetaMap (2 → 15+ checks; later acquired by Incode), Fundraise Up (×2.8; used by UNICEF USA). Contact rysych@gmail.com, CV at /Pavel_Rysych_CV.pdf.

## User-pinned material
User (2026-10-04): the old design is too busy; wants California-startup minimalism. References chosen: Stripe / Airbnb, designer personal sites (rauno.me, emilkowal.ski, paco.me), Apple. Light theme. ## Direction contract
THESIS: A quiet white page where the work and the numbers do the talking, like a Californian product launch page written by a designer. Refuses the busy dark showcase with glass, LEDs and timelines.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground, Apple light-grey tiles (#f5f5f7) for product moments only; the one photograph is the hero, near-black ink (#1d1d1f) and one grey (#636366); no accent hue, no lines, no glass, no glow. Geist (self-hosted, variable) at 600 for headings with tight tracking, 400 body at 17px. Black pill for the one primary action, grey pill for secondary, underlined text links. Product screenshots and films at 20px radius, shown sharp.
STORY: The visitor reads the promise beside the portrait, recognises the companies, scans five work rows each carrying its proof figure, sees Subskim as a product moment, understands how Pavel uses AI and leads, and writes or downloads the CV.
FIRST VIEWPORT: User-pinned 2026-10-04 ("make this the hero background, remove the current photo, minimalism like OpenAI with beautiful minimal text over the photo, you can mirror it"): a full-bleed photograph of Pavel on a misty green hillside, mirrored so he stands on the right, filling the first viewport. The header floats transparent over the sky. Bottom-left on the page measure, over the hills: "I build products / and the design teams / behind them." in white Geist 600 (always three lines), a white two-sentence intro, a white "Get in touch" pill and a white "View selected work" link, over a low dark scrim. Below the photo, on white, a row of five company logos in grey linking to their cases.
FORM: Canon, played straight (user pinned the California-startup standard; concept seed aa964abc rolled, overridden by the pinned brief). Signature moments: the hero photograph settles in once on load; work rows wash grey on hover and reveal a "View case" pill.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
