In brief

A venture studio runs several businesses at once, each at its own stage. Across four of them, my job was the same: find the decision each customer needed to make, and make it obvious.

The problem

No single playbook

Mechanism Ventures builds and develops companies. As Head of Product Design there, I worked on the launch and ongoing development of Pettable, Learner, Top Nutrition Coaching and PrimePutt.

Each was at a different stage and asked something different of design: give an early idea a usable form, make a first experience clear, or improve a journey people were already using. A pattern that worked for one could be wrong for the next.

The approach

Start from the customer’s decision

The work spanned early MVPs, onboarding and improvements to existing customer journeys. Moving between businesses, I started each time from the product’s stage and the one decision its customer needed to make next — then designed the shortest clear path to it.

The result

Clearer first steps, fewer people leaving

At Pettable, onboarding conversion grew by 28% within three months. Leave-intent interventions reduced user churn by 18%.

For context, not credit: the Mechanism Ventures portfolio drives more than $300M in revenue.

The takeaway

Adapt the work to the venture

That range shaped how I work with startups today: get close to the customer, make the important decision obvious and keep the experience coherent as the business grows.
