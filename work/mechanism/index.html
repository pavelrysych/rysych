The story / In brief

One venture studio. Four businesses at different stages, each with its own customer and product challenge.

01 / CONTEXT

Work across the portfolio

Mechanism Ventures builds and develops companies. As a Product Designer there, I worked on the launch and ongoing development of Pettable, Learner, Top Nutrition Coaching and PrimePutt.

The work spanned early MVPs, onboarding and improvements to existing customer journeys. Moving between businesses meant understanding the product stage and the decision each customer needed to make.

03 / TAKEAWAY

Adapt the work to the venture

Across these products, my design work had to meet different needs: give an early idea a usable form, make a first experience clear, or improve a journey people were already using.

That range shaped how I work with startups today: get close to the customer, make the important decision obvious and keep the experience coherent as the business grows.
