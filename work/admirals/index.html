The story / In brief

I aligned customer strategy, design leadership and web production around experienced traders.

01 / DIRECTION

Start with the customer

The shift toward experienced traders gave the work a shared direction. It connected questions about positioning and acquisition with the journeys people experienced after arriving at the product.

My role was to bring design into those decisions early and help the team choose which customer problems deserved attention.

02 / EXPERIENCE

Make the proposition visible

I led the redesign of the company’s web experience around a clearer account of whom Admirals serves. The work covered customer journeys, information, visual direction and the path from interest to action.

The redesigned website is live. It offers a concrete view of the direction, while the underlying customer and commercial outcomes belong to a wider cross-functional effort.

03 / TEAM

Build more ownership

I reshaped the design team around senior designers with more responsibility for decisions and execution. Research, behavioral analytics and experimentation became part of how problems were understood and prioritized.

The aim was a team that could explain its choices, challenge assumptions and carry work through to a stronger result.

04 / AI IN PRACTICE

Change the production workflow

I reorganized web production from a workflow supported by six developers to two designers using Claude and one developer. I configured Claude with marketing strategy, design direction and brand guidelines so the generated work had the necessary context.

AI also helped audit years of product and web legacy, identify repeated patterns, document components and interaction states, and compare implementation with the design system through Figma MCP. I used Lovable for working prototypes and a creative compliance checker, and ran workshops to help colleagues apply the tools.
