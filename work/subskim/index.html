The story / In brief

A subscription tracker that makes recurring payments visible and renewal decisions easier.

01 / THE NEED

Make recurring costs visible

The product groups subscriptions by status, billing cycle, category and spending. A calendar surfaces what renews next, while a periodic review helps people decide what still earns its place.

Its visual language is calm and direct because the task is about recognizing patterns and making choices, not adding another layer of noise.

02 / AI IN THE PRODUCT

Import, then review

AI-assisted screenshot import can suggest subscription details from an image. The extracted information is shown to the user for review before it is saved.

That confirmation step matters: a screenshot can be incomplete or misread, and the person remains responsible for what enters their subscription list.

03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Support an ongoing habit

Subskim combines a command center, renewal calendar and monthly review so that subscription management continues after the initial setup.

The experience links the first import to a recurring review: notice a charge, confirm its details and decide what to do before the next renewal.
