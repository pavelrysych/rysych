The story / In brief

I led design as a growing verification platform expanded beyond its early offering.

01 / PRODUCT

Design for expanding complexity

I led design through a product pivot that broadened the platform’s verification offering. The work required clearer journeys and reusable patterns as customers gained more ways to configure and use verification.

The challenge was to preserve understandable decisions and a coherent experience while the product’s possibilities multiplied.

02 / TEAM

Give the team shared tools

I led the design team and helped establish a more consistent way of working. We moved the design process into Figma and brought analytics into product discussions.

That foundation made it easier to compare options, explain decisions and collaborate with product and engineering on a changing platform.

03 / LEADERSHIP

Keep direction connected to delivery

The role joined product direction with the daily work of designers and developers. Design processes were valuable when they helped people ship coherent work across many verification scenarios.

As the offering expanded, I kept the design process close to the product decisions it needed to support.
