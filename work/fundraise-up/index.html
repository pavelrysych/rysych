The story / In brief

As founding designer, I worked across the donation experience and early product foundations.

01 / FOUNDATION

Make the product real

I designed across the core experience and early product surfaces, helping turn a promising concept into something people could actually use.

In a small team, the work moved between interface detail, product priorities and communication without a large structure around it.

02 / DONATION FLOW

Work on the moment of giving

The donation journey was central: the interaction had to be clear and simple enough to support a real decision, while giving nonprofits a useful product to offer supporters.

I also worked on the product surfaces used by nonprofits. The campaign overview brought donations, recurring gifts and supporters into one clear operating view.

03 / GAMIFICATION

Small interactions. A much easier way to give.

I used gamification and motion to guide the donation journey: a clear choice between one-time and monthly giving, responsive amount selection and small moments of positive feedback. This increased conversion into monthly payments and made recurring giving easy to understand from the start.

The donation button carried the experience from processing to a clear success state. Immediate animated feedback reduced the perceived wait and removed the feeling of a long loading screen while the payment was being processed.

The overall donation conversion increased 2.8×, with donor satisfaction at around 98% CSAT. The compact experience helped a small company compete with much larger, established platforms whose donation journeys relied on large, lengthy forms.

The established pattern

A donation should not feel like paperwork.

Larger competitors often presented donation details, donor information, billing and payment fields together in one long form. Donors had to face all the work before making their first decision.

Our design focused attention on the next useful choice and used feedback to keep the journey moving. That clarity became a competitive advantage for a much smaller company.

04 / EARLY-STAGE DESIGN

Build beyond the screen

Founding design included early pitch and marketing materials as well as the interface itself. That breadth taught me how design can help a company explain what it is building before its product and team are fully formed.

That early work connected the experience of giving with the tools nonprofits needed to understand and manage support.
