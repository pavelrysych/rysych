AI in practice

As a design leader, I work on the context, standards and team practices that make AI useful in everyday work.

Web production

I reorganized web production from a workflow supported by six developers to two designers using Claude and one developer. Designers took ideas through to working interfaces, with developer support.

  1. ConfigureClaude with marketing strategy, design direction and brand guidelines
  2. Hand offWorking interfaces to one developer for implementation
  3. TeachWorkshops to help colleagues apply the tools

Design system to code

I used AI to audit years of product and web legacy and find duplicated patterns, then documented components, semantic tokens and interaction states. Figma MCP gave Claude the design context to compare implementation with the system.

  1. AuditYears of product and web legacy for duplicated patterns
  2. DocumentComponents, semantic tokens and interaction states
  3. CompareImplementation with the design system through Figma MCP

Compliance checker

A tool I built to support the compliance review of marketing creatives. I used AI to turn the brief, design direction and brand context into a working interface that could be tried and refined.

Direction. Ownership. Craft.

Across my projects the pattern is the same: find the problem that matters, make the next decision obvious and measure what changes. I connect business priorities, customer needs and design quality, giving the team a clear direction and the autonomy to deliver.

Set a clear direction
I align the team around who we are building for and how we will measure success. At Admirals, that meant experienced traders instead of “easy start” promises, and Client Business Value instead of the number of clients. Read the Admirals bet
Give designers ownership
I give designers context, clear responsibilities and room to make decisions. At Admirals, I reshaped the design team around senior designers with more responsibility for decisions and execution. How the team changed
Stay close to the work
I stay involved in product flows, prototypes, design systems and implementation. At Admirals, I redesigned the Sign Up flow from one screen to seven. Watch the Sign Up redesign

A wider view of design

My career has taken me from founding designer to leading design teams, with responsibilities extending into product and growth. I understand the customer, choose the right problem and help a team deliver a thoughtful solution.

Much of my work sits at moments when people need confidence to act: making a donation, verifying their identity or choosing a financial service. Clarity and trust are recurring themes in that work.

Outside product design, I explore photography and filmmaking — other ways of working with attention, detail and storytelling.
