// 03 Applied intelligence

AI in
practice.

I use AI to explore ideas, support implementation and change how design teams work. The value is in the decisions, context and review around the tools.

01 / PROTOTYPE

Make ideas tangible.

At Admirals, I used Lovable to prototype ideas and built a tool to support creative compliance review. I gave Claude marketing, design and brand context to work within.

02 / SYSTEM

Connect design to code.

I used AI to audit product and web legacy, develop reusable components, semantic tokens and states, and pass Figma design context into implementation.

03 / TEAM

Change how work ships.

I reorganized web production around two designers using Claude and one developer, and ran workshops to bring practical AI workflows to colleagues.

04 / PRODUCT

Keep the human in control.

In Subskim, AI helps extract subscription details from screenshots. People review the suggested information before anything is saved.

// 05 About

A wider view
of design.

My career has taken me from founding designer to leading design teams, with responsibilities extending into product and growth. I understand the customer, choose the right problem and help a team deliver a thoughtful solution.

Much of my work sits at moments when people need confidence to act: making a donation, verifying their identity or choosing a financial service. Clarity and trust are recurring themes in that work.

Outside product design, I explore photography and filmmaking — other ways of working with attention, detail and storytelling.
