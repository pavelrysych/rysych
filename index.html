Direction. Ownership. Craft.

Across every project the pattern is the same: find the problem that matters, make the next decision obvious and measure what changes. I connect business priorities, customer needs and design quality, giving the team a clear direction and the autonomy to deliver.

Set a clear direction
I align the team around who we are building for, which problem matters and how we will measure success.
Give designers ownership
I give designers context, clear responsibilities and room to make decisions, with feedback and support as they grow.
Stay close to the work
I stay involved in product flows, prototypes, design systems and implementation, helping the team carry good decisions through to the finished product.

A wider view of design

My career has taken me from founding designer to leading design teams, with responsibilities extending into product and growth. I understand the customer, choose the right problem and help a team deliver a thoughtful solution.

Much of my work sits at moments when people need confidence to act: making a donation, verifying their identity or choosing a financial service. Clarity and trust are recurring themes in that work.

Outside product design, I explore photography and filmmaking — other ways of working with attention, detail and storytelling.
